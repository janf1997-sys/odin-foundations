Butter Tofu

Description

The ultimate Indian comfort food. Quick and easy to make.

Ingredients

For the Tofu

  • 400 g tofu
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 tbsp curry powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt

For the Sauce

  • 2 tbsp vegan butter
  • 1 large onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
  • 1 tbsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 90 g tomato paste
  • 400 g coconut milk or plant-based cooking cream

For Serving

  • Rice
  • Naan
  • Fresh coriander
  • Limes
  • Sesame seeds

Steps

  1. Wrap the tofu in paper towels and press dry as best as you can. Then unwrap and tear into cube-sized pieces.
  2. Place the tofu chunks in a bowl. Add the cornstarch and seasonings and toss until all the pieces are coated.
  3. Heat the oil in a frying pan. Add the tofu pieces and cook until all sides are lightly browned and crispy. Remove the tofu and set aside.
  4. Wipe out the pan with a paper towel. Add the vegan butter and heat up. Add the onions and sauté for 3-4 minutes until translucent. Then add the garlic and ginger and sauté for a further minute. Add the spices and tomato paste and stir-fry briefly. Then add the coconut milk, stir and simmer for approx. 5 minutes.
  5. Add the tofu to the sauce and stir gently until all the pieces are coated.
  6. Serve the butter tofu with rice and vegan naan. Garnish with fresh coriander, lime and sesame seeds. Enjoy your meal!
