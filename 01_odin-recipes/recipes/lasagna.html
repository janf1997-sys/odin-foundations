Vegan Lasagna

Description

This tasty vegan lasagna is the perfect comfort food on a cold day.

Ingredients

Bolognese Sauce

  • 3 tbsp olive oil
  • 500 g vegan mince
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, chopped
  • 2 stalks celery, chopped
  • 120 ml red wine (optional)
  • 400 g passata
  • 400 g diced tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp Italian seasoning
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Béchamel Sauce

  • 30 g vegan butter
  • 30 g all-purpose flour
  • 500 ml unsweetened soy milk
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 pinch ground nutmeg (optional)
  • 2 tbsp nutritional yeast (optional)

Assembly

  • 9-12 no-boil lasagna noodles

Toppings (Optional)

  • Vegan shredded cheese
  • Vegan parmesan
  • Fresh basil, chopped

Steps

Bolognese Sauce

  1. Heat 1-2 tablespoons of oil in a non-stick pan and fry the vegan mince according to package directions until crumbly. Then set aside.
  2. Heat the remaining oil in a large deep pan or pot. Add the onions, carrots and celery and sauté for 2-3 minutes until the onions are translucent. Then add the garlic and sauté for 30 seconds. Next, add tomato paste and italian herbs and sauté for a few seconds.
  3. Now stir in the vegan mince. Then deglaze with red wine and let it boil down briefly. Now stir in diced tomatoes and passata, season with a little salt and pepper and simmer the Bolognese sauce for about 15 minutes with a lid on it until it has thickened well. Taste and adjust seasonings as needed and allow to cool slightly.

Béchamel Sauce

  1. Melt the vegan butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Then wisk in the flour and sweat for 1 minute, stirring constantly. Now slowly add the soy milk and bring to a boil, continuing to stir until the sauce thickens. Season to taste with salt, pepper, nutmeg and nutritional yeast. Then remove from heat and set aside.

Layer the lasagna

  1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C. Brush a baking dish with a little oil.
  2. First spread a thin layer of Bolognese sauce on the bottom of the baking dish. Then alternate layers of lasagna sheets, bolognese and béchamel on top. Finish with a thick layer of béchamel sauce. Finally, sprinkle with some vegan grated cheese and/or vegan Parmesan and bake the lasagna for about 45 minutes.
  3. Let the lasagna cool down a bit while it becomes more sliceable. Then garnish with fresh basil and enjoy!
