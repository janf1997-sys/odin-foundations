Chili Garlic Crispy Tofu

Home

Description

Crispy, garlicy and just enough spice to keep you warm on a cold day.

Ingredients

For the Tofu

  • 1 block extra firm tofu
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp cornstarch
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 70 g breadcrumbs

For the Sauce

  • 50 g sugar
  • 80 ml water
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp chili garlic sauce
  • 1 tbsp sriracha
  • 2 tsp garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 2 tsp cornstarch

Steps

  1. Remove the tofu from package and drain off the excess liquid.
  2. Place on a flat surface with a towel on top and place a heavy object on top. Press for 20-30 minutes.
  3. Cut or tear into bite-sized cubes.
  4. Preheat the oven to 200 degrees Celcius
  5. Toss the tofu with the soy sauce, then the cornstarch, garlic powder and breadcrumbs.
  6. Place on a baking sheet lined with parchment papaer. Try to keep each piece evenly spaced to ensure they're all able to get crispy.
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes or until golden brown and crispy.
  8. Meanwhile, whisk together all ingredients for the sauce in a small pan on the stove.
  9. Bring to a low boil, then let simmer, whisking occasionally, until thick.
  10. Remove from the heat.
  11. Let the tofu cool for a few minutes once it has baked, then toss with the sauce and serve immediately.
  12. I served with jasmine rice, steamed broccoli, avocado and carrots. Enjoy!
